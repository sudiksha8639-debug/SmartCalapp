## 📌 Overview

SmartCal is an AI-powered health and nutrition assistant designed especially for students. It helps users make healthier lifestyle choices by providing personalized nutrition insights, calorie estimation, hydration tracking, wellness reminders, and AI-powered health recommendations.

The goal of SmartCal is to simplify healthy living using Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

# 💡 Why SmartCal?

Many students struggle to maintain a healthy lifestyle due to busy schedules and lack of personalized guidance.

SmartCal bridges this gap by combining Artificial Intelligence with nutrition tracking to deliver practical and personalized health recommendations.
